# EdgeLog Brand Guide

**Tagline:** Log Bets. Gain Edge.

## Brand idea

The EdgeLog symbol combines a geometric **E**, an ascending performance line, and analytics bars. It represents disciplined tracking and data-driven improvement without casino imagery.

## Primary colors

- Electric Blue — `#2563FF`
- Cyan — `#00D4FF`
- Violet — `#7C3AED`
- Navy — `#0B1220`
- Deep Navy — `#07101F`
- Slate — `#94A3B8`
- White — `#FFFFFF`

Primary gradient:

```css
linear-gradient(135deg, #7C3AED 0%, #2563FF 48%, #00D4FF 100%)
```

## Assets

- `assets/edgelog-mark.svg` — transparent primary symbol and favicon
- `assets/edgelog-app-icon.svg` — rounded app icon
- `assets/edgelog-horizontal.svg` — dark-background horizontal lockup

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least 20% of its width.
- Minimum digital symbol size: 24 px.
- Minimum horizontal lockup width: 160 px.
- Use the gradient version as the primary mark.
- Use monochrome variants when gradients are unsuitable.

## Avoid

- Stretching or rotating the symbol
- Adding bevels or heavy glow effects
- Changing the symbol proportions
- Placing it on a visually noisy background
