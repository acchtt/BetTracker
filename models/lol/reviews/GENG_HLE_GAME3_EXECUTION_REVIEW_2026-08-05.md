## What was still missed

### 1. Draft execution burden

The pregame assessment gave HLE a moderate draft edge from frontline and scaling. It underweighted Gen.G's simpler repeatable execution:

- Varus long-range initiation;
- Nocturne information denial and delivery;
- Syndra burst and pick conversion;
- Kalista-Renata lane pressure, protection, engage layering, and objective control.

HLE's carries had a higher protection and positioning burden. Mundo's top-side value could not automatically compensate for a losing mid-and-bot damage core.

### 2. No immediate full market rescan after correction

The role-gold correction invalidated the prior HLE-side lean. The model withdrew that lean but did not immediately rescan the visible moneyline, handicap, total, and duration prices before performing repository work and requesting another screenshot.

The correct process was:

1. repair the orientation;
2. regrade all visible markets from the same synchronized state;
3. issue a replacement verdict;
4. perform model maintenance afterward.

This does not mean Gen.G -3.5 was automatically official at 2-2. It still required four future net kills and additional margin confirmation. It does mean the Gen.G side and negative handicap should have been re-opened as candidates immediately.

### 3. Execution quality lacked an explicit score

The model treated execution descriptively rather than as a live signal capable of overriding the draft prior.

Gen.G's repeated conversion into four dragons, two Barons, and nine towers showed an execution advantage that should progressively upgrade winner and margin assessments.

### 4. Margin expansion was not linked strongly enough to carry concentration

The early Gen.G carry-concentrated gold lead was relevant to future kill separation, provided target access and protection remained functional. The model used role gold mainly for winner direction and did not fully connect it to the handicap family.

### 5. Kill-total projection was too low

The early point estimate was approximately 33 kills, with Under 34.5 treated as a watch. The final total was 41.

The underestimation came from insufficient weight on:

- two-sided engage and return-kill routes;
- repeated Varus-Nocturne-Syndra pick access;
- HLE's ability to trade kills through Leona, Shyvana, Mundo, Viktor, and Yunara;
- repeated major-objective contests;
- the possibility that a superior execution team wins through repeated contested sequences rather than a clean low-kill close.

The result shows that execution superiority can expand both the winner's margin and the total-kill inventory.
