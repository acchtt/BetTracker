## Draft evaluation

Every complete draft must be scored for role fit, lane priority, independent damage channels, damage delivery, frontline versus actual opposing damage, engage-follow-up geometry, peel, carry protection, side-lane and waveclear, objective setup, execution burden, and single-point-of-failure risk.

Do not call a composition stable solely because it contains a tank, engage, and a scaling carry. Explicitly verify that the damage dealers can operate safely and that the engage plan does not isolate the primary carry.

For Sylas, assess lane access, combat-ultimate quality, flank dependence, target access, and whether he is a reliable secondary damage source. For Varus or another immobile primary carry, assess protection, opposing access, damage uptime, and whether the draft has another dependable ranged damage channel.

User-facing draft verdicts remain verdict-first and concise. Full lane-by-lane detail may follow.

## Moneyline lean and official gates

For a live underdog moneyline:

- after the opening state, draft edge alone cannot support a lean;
- `LEAN — 0u watch` requires one hard confirmation or two genuinely independent soft confirmations;
- against a substantial team-quality gap, at least one hard confirmation is required even for a lean;
- two independent current-map confirmations remain mandatory for official promotion;
- at least one confirmation must be hard under v0.3.17;
- kills plus their immediate resulting gold remain one correlated cluster;
- Void Grubs without meaningful structure conversion are soft confirmation;
- kills, small gold and Grubs cannot complete the official gate by themselves;
- the analysis must price lead-conversion reliability, the next two likely sequences, the favorite's comeback routes and the most realistic throw branch;
- when the pre-match market, series context or observed play indicates a substantial team-quality gap, official promotion requires two hard confirmations or one hard plus two additional independent confirmations.

Hard confirmation includes structure conversion, dragon control with maintained setup, Herald converted into material structure damage, a second post-reset structured-fight win, a usable item breakpoint with map control, or repeated wave/vision/terrain control that denies a normal contest.

## Positive-underdog kill handicaps

Before issuing any positive-underdog kill-handicap lean, verify:

- functioning item-qualified return damage;
- target access, counter-engage, cleanup, or other credible return-kill routes;
- no unresolved carry or role collapse;
- structural and objective deficits do not imply repeated forced entries into prepared terrain;
- the cushion survives a conservative next-fight stress test.

A wider number or higher price cannot replace functioning damage or map access.

Official promotion still requires two qualifying structured-fight conversions plus the full damage, structure, item, and reliability gates.

## Same-map thesis lockout

Maintain one active thesis per map and market family.

If a thesis is withdrawn for functional collapse, lost objective control, failed damage delivery, or invalidated conversion assumptions, re-entry is prohibited until:

1. two new qualifying structured-fight conversions occur;
2. structural or objective stabilization is demonstrated;
3. item-qualified damage or frontline function is restored;
4. the original failure condition is explicitly shown to be repaired.

A wider line, one isolated kill, improved odds, or one opponent mistake does not unlock re-entry.

## Kill totals

Kill-Over leans require observed two-sided structured-fight conversion or a demonstrated high-velocity kill window, functioning damage on both teams, feasible remaining kill requirements, and sufficient mandatory contests. Draft engage density alone is not enough.

At or after 15:00, decision-critical item strength must be known.

Kill Unders retain all v0.3.12 headroom, buffer, fight-budget, and one-fight-close gates. In addition, score the trailing team's return-kill production, target access, forced-entry density, mandatory contests, and likely finish path.
