# LoL v0.3.25 Pre-Match Preview and Pre-Game Procedure

**Status:** Active immediately  
**Effective:** 2026-08-04 11:13 UTC+7  
**Purpose:** Separate series-level pre-match previews from map-specific pre-game analysis and prevent side-selection checks from being applied at the wrong stage.

## 1. Terminology

Use these terms precisely:

- **Pre-match preview** — analysis of the upcoming match or series before a specific map is being prepared.
- **Pre-game analysis** — analysis of a specific map, such as Game 1, Game 2, or Game 3, including the current series state and draft when available.

Do not use the two procedures interchangeably.

## 2. Pre-match preview procedure

A pre-match preview establishes the series-level baseline.

Verify and assess:

1. teams, competition, match date, and series format;
2. expected rosters, substitutions, and material availability concerns;
3. pre-match moneyline, series handicap, correct-score, or other available series-level markets;
4. market-implied team-quality gap;
5. recent form and strength of opposition;
6. recurring early-game, objective-control, teamfight, side-lane, and lead-conversion tendencies;
7. likely strategic matchup and the main conditions that could alter the market baseline;
8. which markets should be revisited after a specific map draft is known.

### Side-selection rule

**Do not require or verify side selection for a pre-match preview.**

Side selection belongs to the specific-map pre-game procedure. Its absence must not delay, downgrade, or block a series-level preview.

A pre-match preview may mention broad historical blue-side or red-side tendencies only when relevant, but must not assume which side either team will receive in an upcoming map.

## 3. Pre-match preview output

Use this order:

1. series-level verdict or baseline first;
2. market-implied team-quality prior;
3. principal matchup advantages and risks;
4. likely series paths;
5. market consequences;
6. what must be checked again at the map-specific pre-game stage.

Do not present a post-draft map verdict during the pre-match preview.

## 4. Specific-map pre-game procedure

For Game 1, Game 2, Game 3, or another identified map, verify:

1. exact game number;
2. current series score;
3. side selection when available;
4. complete draft and correct team-to-champion orientation;
5. exact map moneyline, kill handicap, kill total, and duration markets;
6. whether each market is open, delayed, suspended, or unavailable;
7. any user correction to roster, side, draft, price, or market orientation.

If side selection is not yet available, state that it is unverified and avoid side-dependent assumptions. Continue with information that does not depend on side selection, but do not claim a verified side-specific edge.

## 5. Mandatory post-draft scorecard

Once the full draft is available, assess:

1. role and lane fit;
2. lane priority, volatility, and punishability by role;
3. independent reliable damage channels;
4. damage delivery, range, uptime, safety, and target access;
5. frontline durability against the opponent's actual damage profile;
6. engage reliability and follow-up geometry;
7. peel, disengage, counter-engage, and carry protection;
8. pick strength versus front-to-back teamfight quality;
9. side-lane pressure, waveclear, and map-assignment flexibility;
10. objective setup, choke control, and terrain dependence;
11. execution burden and major failure states;
12. single-point-of-failure risk.

Also identify:

- the simpler win condition;
- the side requiring more precise sequencing;
- the most punishable champion on each team;
- the first composition likely to become nonfunctional if one role falls behind;
- carry-isolation or damage-concentration risk;
- whether frontline and engage actually preserve damage delivery.

## 6. Map-market translation

After the draft, separately assess:

1. **Moneyline** — overall winning probability and conversion reliability.
2. **Kill handicap** — expected final margin, independently from winner confidence.
3. **Kill total** — two-sided fight production, return-kill capacity, and clean-close risk.
4. **Duration** — snowball speed, scaling, waveclear, objective cycles, and conversion burden.

All current v0.3.25 eligibility, minimum-odds, exposure, taxonomy, placement-confirmation, and duration-quarantine rules remain active.

## 7. Specific-map pre-game output

Use this order:

1. exact verdict first;
2. verified game number, series score, and side selection status;
3. directional draft edge;
4. simpler win condition;
5. principal damage-delivery advantage;
6. main vulnerable champion or failure point;
7. moneyline, kill handicap, kill total, and duration conclusions;
8. exact line, odds, stake, eligibility, and placement status when actionable.

## 8. Transition into live analysis

The pre-match preview establishes the team-quality prior. The specific-map pre-game analysis adds side and draft information. Neither is permanent once live evidence arrives.

At the first synchronized live state:

- verify score orientation and state direction;
- test whether predicted draft functions are actually operating;
- assess all four market families;
- prefer current-map evidence over the pre-match or pre-game thesis when they conflict;
- apply explicit user corrections over stale telemetry.
