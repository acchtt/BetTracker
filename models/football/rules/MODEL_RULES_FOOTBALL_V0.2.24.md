# MODEL RULES — FOOTBALL v0.2.24

Effective: 2026-08-04

This version supplements v0.2.23 and all active football procedures.

## 1. LEAN stake floor

Effective immediately:

- the minimum recommended stake for any football LEAN is **0.25u = 250,000 VND**;
- the former 0.125u LEAN — SMALL tier is retired for future recommendations;
- because the normal official football stake cap remains 0.25u, every executable LEAN now uses exactly **0.25u**;
- WATCH and NO BET classifications carry no stake;
- a wager remains official only after confirmed placement.

Recommendation labels remain:

- **OFFICIAL BET** for a confirmed placed wager;
- **LEAN** for an executable model recommendation at 0.25u;
- **NO BET** when no qualifying edge exists.

Do not use **LEAN — SMALL** in future football recommendations unless this stake policy is explicitly changed again.

## 2. Non-retroactivity

This stake-policy change does not retroactively alter:

- previously issued recommendations;
- historical shadow grading;
- confirmed settled stakes;
- existing ledger entries.

Any open wager whose stake has not yet been explicitly confirmed remains stake-unconfirmed until the user supplies the amount or slip evidence.

## 3. Existing controls unchanged

All other active controls remain unchanged, including:

- 1u = 1,000,000 VND;
- minimum accepted odds = 1.70;
- normal official football stake cap = 0.25u;
- every material score, minute, line, odds, card, substitution, injury, penalty, weather or pitch change requires independent repricing;
- synchronized two-team snapshot requirements;
- market-line trajectory analysis;
- exact settlement analysis;
- ledger.json remains authoritative;
- ledger updates remain on hold until explicitly approved.
