# League of Legends Model Rules v0.3.4

**Status:** Active evaluation addendum  
**Effective date:** 2026-07-31  
**Applies to:** LoL post-draft and live kill handicaps, moneylines, totals, and duration markets  
**Read with:** `LOL_BETTING_PROCEDURE.md`, prior active LoL addenda, and `ledger.json`

This addendum strengthens draft damage evaluation. It does not relax team-strength, synchronization, stake, or market-selection rules.

## 1. Engage is not damage conversion

Do not treat reliable initiation, crowd control, zone control, or wave-clear as proof that a team can trade kills.

A composition may start fights well yet fail to finish targets because follow-up damage arrives late, the engager outruns the carries, damage is front-loaded without sustained DPS, enemy shields or peel absorb the first rotation, frontline kill speed is too low, or target access depends on one narrow ultimate or flank.

## 2. Mandatory engage-to-damage conversion gate

Before recommending a positive kill handicap or underdog moneyline based on fight setup, verify:

1. at least two credible champion-damage sources, excluding the primary tank engager;
2. damage timing aligned with the control chain;
3. realistic target access without immediate counter-engage punishment;
4. credible frontline kill speed when backline access fails;
5. sustained output after the first spell rotation;
6. protection and positioning that allow carry uptime;
7. player proficiency and role fit supporting the theoretical path.

Failure on two or more items normally caps a positive kill handicap at `NO BET`, even when initiation is clearly superior.

## 3. Wave-clear separation

Wave-clear improves stall probability and map survival. It is not direct evidence of champion-damage conversion.

- Do not count wave-clear or zone denial as kill-trading evidence unless those champions can also maintain damage uptime in fights.
- When wave-clear is strong but fight damage is weak, assess duration and total kills independently rather than defaulting to an underdog kill handicap.
- Pass when no alternative market has a clear edge.

## 4. Damage profile versus opposing structure

Evaluate damage against the actual opposing composition:

- sustained DPS versus tanks and shields;
- burst reliability versus peel and protection;
- melee access versus displacement and counter-engage;
- damage spread when one carry is zoned or removed;
- relative long-fight scaling.

A team with good setup but only one dependable follow-up source receives a larger downside tail for one-sided fight chains.

## 5. Live validation

For live positive kill handicaps, require observed conversion rather than draft theory alone. At least one of the following should be visible before upgrading to `OFFICIAL BET`:

- an engage producing proportionate damage or a kill trade;
- relevant role-level damage-item breakpoints;
- objective fights with sustained carry uptime;
- evidence that the opposing frontline is being meaningfully threatened.

Repeated clean setups with little follow-up damage are a negative signal and trigger an immediate downgrade.

## 6. Market-selection consequence

When initiation is strong but conversion is doubtful:

- downgrade the underdog positive kill handicap;
- reassess the favorite moneyline and favorite kill-margin path;
- assess total kills and duration separately;
- do not force an alternate wager solely because the handicap was rejected.

## 7. Triggering case

KT Rolster Challengers vs DN SOOPers Challengers, Game 1, 2026-07-31:

- DNS.C drafted Rumble, Skarner, Locke, Sivir, and Alistar.
- The model leaned DNS.C +4.5 kills because Skarner-Alistar initiation, Rumble zoning, and Sivir wave-clear appeared to support kill trading.
- DNS.C created setups but could not produce reliable follow-up damage into K'Sante, Shen, Lee Sin disruption, Sylas, and Cassiopeia sustained damage.
- KT.C won 26-7 in kills, 70.5k-56.5k gold, and 10-2 towers in 33:35.
- The error was draft evaluation, not wager synchronization: the accepted same-line price improved from 1.677 to 1.806 with no reported intervening action.
