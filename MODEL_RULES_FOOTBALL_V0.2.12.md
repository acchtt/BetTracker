# Football Model Rules v0.2.12

**Effective date:** 2026-07-31  
**Status:** Active operational restriction and evaluation addendum  
**Supersedes:** Older football rules where they conflict. `MODEL_RULES_FOOTBALL_V0.2.11.md` remains active for corner-market scanning, `MODEL_RULES_FOOTBALL_V0.2.10.md` for recommendation tiers and exposure, and `MODEL_RULES_FOOTBALL_V0.2.9.md` for live quote validity.

## Trigger

A football-ledger review identified recurring process failures: quiet snapshots were extrapolated too far, stoppage-time and late-game tails were underpriced, knockout incentives were reduced to one narrative, correlated chance indicators were double-counted, brittle lines were selected too often, and placed leans were mixed with official performance.

## Active corrections

### 1. Persistence gate for live state

A single screenshot cannot establish a persistent trend.

Before using suppression or pressure as a major probability input, require at least one of:

- two synchronized snapshots separated by meaningful game time;
- a documented 8–15 minute trend;
- reliable event-sequence data showing repeated independent attacking or defensive phases.

Without persistence evidence, apply a material uncertainty penalty and normally cap the output at `LEAN — WATCH` or `NO BET`.

### 2. Brittle live-under restriction

Do not issue an `OFFICIAL BET` on full-match Under 1.5 when more than approximately 55 regulation minutes remain if any of the following has occurred:

- a big chance;
- a penalty or material VAR incident;
- a red card;
- an injury or substitution that materially changes attacking structure;
- a confirmed high-tempo or transition-heavy phase;
- uncertain stoppage-time exposure.

An exception requires exceptional suppression confirmed by multiple snapshots, no material attacking-state warning, and an uncertainty-adjusted edge that remains above the official threshold after the late-goal reserve.

For similar long-horizon unders, prefer protected Under 1.75, Under 2, Under 2.25, or Under 2.75 lines when the settlement-weighted value remains reasonable.

### 3. Explicit late-goal and stoppage-time reserve

Every live total and handicap must include a separate late-goal reserve through expected regulation stoppage time.

The reserve must increase when any of the following applies:

- one-goal margin;
- trailing team must attack;
- multiple substitutions or VAR delays;
- injuries or time-wasting;
- attacking fullbacks or added aerial targets;
- red cards or fatigue;
- qualification, relegation, or elimination pressure;
- leading team has transition space.

Do not model minute 90 as the effective settlement horizon. From minute 75 onward, require a larger robust edge and show the assumed stoppage-time branch.

### 4. Independent pathway requirement

For a wager requiring two or more additional goals, each required goal must have an independently supported pathway.

- A strong next-goal case is not sufficient for a two-goal over.
- Reprice incentives after the next possible scorer.
- Include aggregate-reset, score-protection, and no-goal-to-checkpoint branches.
- If only one additional goal is strongly supported, cap the output at `LEAN — WATCH` or select a protected line with a push or half-settlement boundary.

### 5. Knockout-state discipline

Do not let one aggregate narrative dominate automatically.

For every knockout reassessment, separately price:

- controlled-game branch;
- trailing-team chase branch;
- leading-team counterattack branch;
- aggregate-reset branch after an equalizing goal;
- extra-time acceptance branch when applicable.

A large aggregate lead does not by itself justify an under or underdog handicap. A trailing team needing one goal does not by itself justify a multi-goal over.

### 6. Chance-cluster control

Treat xG, xGOT, big chances, shots on target, box shots, box touches, and corners as partially correlated.

- One attacking passage counts as one chance cluster.
- Do not add a full independent uplift for each metric generated by the same sequence.
- Low values across correlated metrics are also not multiple independent confirmations of suppression.
- Give more weight to repeated independent phases, tactical repeatability, and time persistence than to the number of displayed metrics.

### 7. Protected-line preference

When nearby lines have comparable settlement-weighted value, prefer the line that protects the most plausible boundary outcome.

Examples:

- whole-goal over instead of a quarter-goal over when exactly one more goal is a common branch;
- Under 2.25 or Under 2.75 instead of a brittle half-goal under when the exact boundary has meaningful probability;
- draw-no-bet or positive handicap instead of a moneyline when the draw branch is material.

Do not choose protection solely because it would have helped a prior result; price the protection before entry.

### 8. Cross-league and regional-cup side restriction

Prematch 1X2 and handicap positions in cross-league, regional-cup, youth, reserve, or low-data competitions require:

- confirmed lineups;
- opponent-adjusted team-strength inputs;
- explicit competition-strength uncertainty;
- travel, venue, rotation, and motivation review;
- a robust edge across reasonable league-strength assumptions.

If the edge depends mainly on home advantage, reputation, or one lineup impression, cap the output at `LEAN — WATCH` or `NO BET`.

### 9. Recommendation and exposure discipline

- `OFFICIAL BET`: only when the current v0.2.10 robust-edge gate is satisfied.
- `LEAN — SMALL`: maximum 0.125u and must remain separate from official performance.
- `LEAN — WATCH`: 0u and not actionable until reissued.
- A placed lean never becomes an official model recommendation retroactively.
- Correlated positions in the same match remain subject to the combined exposure cap.

During this correction phase, do not increase football stake sizes above 0.25u. No football martingale, loss-chasing, or stake escalation is permitted.

### 10. Live delivery discipline

For live recommendations, output in this order:

1. recommendation label;
2. exact market and line;
3. executable minimum odds;
4. stake;
5. expiry and invalidation conditions;
6. concise reason.

Detailed analysis follows only after the executable decision. If the quote has changed materially, reassess rather than carrying forward the old edge.

### 11. Process-grade settlement review

Every settled football wager receives two separate grades:

- result grade: win, half win, push, half loss, loss;
- process grade: good, acceptable, mixed, or poor.

A win with a materially mispriced danger tail is not graded as good process. A protected half loss can still be acceptable process if the line selection and probability range were sound.

Track official, lean-small, user override, below-cutoff, oversized, unsynchronized, and imported records separately.

### 12. Mandatory market scan

Every material football assessment must still scan independently:

- 1X2;
- Asian handicap;
- goal totals;
- team totals when reliable;
- corner markets under v0.2.11.

A strong opinion in one market cannot be copied automatically into another.

## Review threshold

Review these corrections after the next 20 settled football wagers or 40 fully documented football scans, whichever comes first.

Report separately by:

- official versus lean-small versus user override;
- prematch versus live;
- totals versus handicaps versus corners;
- domestic versus knockout versus cross-league/regional competition;
- entry minute and score state;
- protected versus brittle line;
- persistence-confirmed versus single-snapshot;
