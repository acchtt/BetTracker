# LoL v0.3.22 Active Rules — Consolidated

LoL v0.3.22 supersedes v0.3.21 where conflicting. All stricter earlier rules remain active.

## Fast verdict rules

- Verdict first.
- Official only after explicit placement confirmation.
- Locked/delayed markets may receive an official model verdict but remain unplaced.
- Minimum odds 1.60.
- Standard probation stake 0.25u.
- One correlated thesis per map.

## Cumulative favorite-margin gate

For a negative kill handicap, calculate the current margin and additional future net kills required.

A live line may be an official recommendation when:

- favorite leads by at least four kills;
- no more than four additional net kills are required;
- favorite has first Baron or equivalent immediate map-control leverage;
- favorite has two functioning damage/conversion channels;
- favorite has reliable backline access;
- opponent lacks durable frontline, reliable disengage, or protected-carry stabilization;
- one opposing primary carry is materially behind or nonfunctional;
- at least three linked exposure windows remain across Baron siege, wave/vision pressure, dragon, next Baron, inhibitor, or base defense;
- conservative cumulative projection clears the requirement by at least one net kill;
- odds are at least 1.60 and state/items are verified.

Exact verdict:

`OFFICIAL BET — CUMULATIVE FAVORITE MARGIN — [market] @ [odds] — 0.25u — not placed`

When suspended:

`MARKET LOCKED/DELAYED — NOT PLACED`

## First-Baron treatment

First Baron normally creates structure pressure, not an automatic ending attempt. Evaluate:

1. first-Baron siege or pick;
2. post-Baron wave and vision catches;
3. dragon or soul-point fight;
4. next Baron, inhibitor, or base-defense sequence.

A reset after first Baron is not thesis failure if map access and future forced exposure remain controlled.

## Positive-handicap inverse

When the cumulative favorite-margin gate is active, the opposing positive handicap is:

`NO BET — CUMULATIVE ATTRITION VETO`

It may repair only after real stabilization: surviving the Baron cycle without meaningful loss, restoring safe waves/vision, winning a structured fight, reducing the deficit near 2k, or restoring a protected item-qualified carry.
