## Fixed operating values

- 1u = 1,000,000 VND.
- Standard stake = 0.25u = 250,000 VND.
- Probation exposure cap = 0.25u per map.
- Minimum accepted odds = 1.60.
- No correlated add-ons during probation.
- A wager becomes official only after user confirmation.
- Recommendations begin with `OFFICIAL BET`, `LEAN`, or `NO BET`.
- Material state or price changes require `EXPIRED — REASSESSMENT REQUIRED`.

## Draft evaluation

Score role fit, lane priority, independent damage channels, damage delivery, frontline versus actual opposing damage, engage-follow-up geometry, peel, carry protection, side-lane and waveclear, objective setup, execution burden, and single-point-of-failure risk.

## Moneylines

- After the opening state, draft edge alone cannot support a live moneyline lean.
- Lean requires one hard confirmation or two independent soft confirmations.
- Against a substantial quality gap, a hard confirmation is required even for a lean.
- Official promotion follows the stricter v0.3.17 gate.

## Positive underdog kill handicaps

- Verify functioning return damage, target access, counter-engage or cleanup, no unresolved carry collapse, structural context, and next-fight stress-test survival.
- Official promotion requires two qualifying structured-fight conversions plus full damage, structure, item, and reliability gates.

## Same-map thesis lockout

One active thesis per map and market family. A withdrawn thesis cannot re-enter until two new qualifying conversions, stabilization, restored functionality, and explicit repair of the original failure condition.

## Kill totals

- Kill Overs require observed two-sided conversion or a demonstrated high-velocity window.
- At or after 15:00, decision-critical item strength must be known.
- Kill Unders retain headroom, fight-budget, safety-buffer, one-fight-close, mandatory-contest, and return-kill-production checks.

## Duration markets

Duration Overs are not eligible for `OFFICIAL BET` during probation wagers 7–10.
