# League of Legends Model Rules v0.3.17

**Status:** Active immediately  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-02 02:33 UTC+7  
**Purpose:** Strengthen live underdog-moneyline promotion after KC vs NAVI Game 1.

## Confirmation hierarchy

### Soft confirmation
- a single kill or first-blood sequence;
- the small gold lead created by that same sequence;
- Void Grubs without meaningful structure conversion;
- isolated CS or lane-priority advantages;
- draft edge without current-map execution;
- one unconverted objective setup;
- one opponent mistake without a repeatable control pattern.

Kills and the resulting immediate gold lead remain one correlated confirmation cluster.

### Hard confirmation
- a tower lead or meaningful structure conversion;
- a dragon lead with maintained setup control;
- Herald converted into material plates, tower damage, or a tower;
- a second structured-fight win after a reset or objective-cycle separation;
- a meaningful, usable item breakpoint on primary damage or frontline combined with map control;
- repeated wave, vision, and terrain control that denies the opponent a normal contest;
- a gold lead large and well distributed enough to remain meaningful after one lost skirmish.

Void Grubs alone are not hard confirmation.

## Underdog live-moneyline official gate

An underdog live moneyline may be `OFFICIAL BET` only when:

1. at least two independent current-map confirmations are present;
2. at least one confirmation is hard;
3. the evidence is not merely kills plus their resulting gold plus Grubs;
4. the underdog has a credible next-two-sequence conversion path;
5. the favorite's comeback routes are explicitly priced;
6. current composition functionality and item strength are checked when required by clock;
7. team/player lead-conversion reliability is not a material unresolved negative.

When there is a substantial team-quality gap, require either two hard confirmations or one hard confirmation plus two additional independent confirmations, including a post-reset fight or objective conversion.

Otherwise, cap the recommendation at `LEAN — 0u watch`.

## Lead-conversion reliability check

Before promotion, state:
- how the lead converts into the next tower, dragon, Herald, Baron setup, or item breakpoint;
- whether the lead is concentrated on champions able to carry the next fight;
- whether disciplined setup, wave preparation, and reset timing have been shown;
- whether the favorite has superior late-game delivery, side-lane pressure, engage reliability, or execution;
- the most realistic throw branch.

## Next-two-sequence test

Return `NO BET` when the most likely path is:
- a low-value objective traded for a tower or dragon;
- inability to convert lane priority into structures;
- the favorite reaches a major item breakpoint first;
- one lost fight erases the entire lead;
- repeated out-execution of a stronger favorite is required without structural insulation.

Attractive odds do not compensate for missing durable conversion.
