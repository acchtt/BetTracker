## Representative thesis

Repeated reprices in one continuous state are one thesis.

Open a new representative thesis only after a material state transition, normally:

- net two-kill swing;
- approximately 1.5k gold movement;
- tower/inhibitor change;
- Baron, soul point, soul, or Elder change;
- decision-critical item breakpoint;
- verified base-access, wave-control, or return-kill-function change.

A price-only change is a reprice update, not a new observation.

## Mandatory four-family scan

Every synchronized snapshot must show:

1. moneyline;
2. kill handicap;
3. kill total;
4. duration.

If absent or suspended, state `MARKET UNAVAILABLE/LOCKED`.

## Winner versus margin

Moneyline confidence does not transfer automatically to a negative kill handicap.

For every negative handicap, state:

- current margin;
- required final margin;
- additional future net kills required;
- structural/objective leverage;
- favorite conversion channels;
- opponent return-kill channels.

Restrictions:

- five or more required future net kills is normally at most `LEAN`;
- -10.5 or larger with current lead below five cannot be official without Baron/equivalent leverage, two-tower lead, two favorite channels, and impaired opponent carry/protection;
- evidence must specifically support margin expansion, not merely the winner.

## Positive-handicap width

Two kills of extra cushion are material.

Do not transfer a +8.5 thesis to +5.5/+6.5 without a fresh calculation. Wider line success does not validate tighter lines. Preserve anti-line-chasing and repaired-watch gates.

## Validated return-kill channels

A validated channel requires:

1. item-qualified or functional damage; and
2. delivery, engage, frontline, peel, target access, or protection.

Damage without executable access/protection is nominal, not validated.

At 18:00+ an official Kill Over requires:

- two leader conversion channels;
- two trailing return-kill channels;
- at least one trailing channel with functional delivery/protection;
- recent two-sided meaningful conversion;
- verified decision-critical items.

One trailing channel means maximum `LEAN`. Zero means `NO BET` on the Over.

## Clean-close veto

Exact verdict:

`NO BET — CLEAN-CLOSE/RETURN-KILL SUPPRESSION VETO`

Mandatory when at least four are present:

1. Baron/base access/inhibitor route or two-tower lead;
2. leader has two item-qualified channels;
3. trailing engage/frontline/peel/protection impaired;
4. trailing side has at most one validated return-kill channel;
5. leader won intervening state by at least 2-0 kills while improving gold/structure/objectives;
6. one credible objective-to-base finish exists before another full neutral cycle.

When active, unresolved map inventory does not justify an Over by itself and all earlier Over theses expire.

## Kill Over promotion

Near parity:

- central projection at least two above line;
- lower conservative branch no more than three below.

Leader-controlled state — any of +3k gold, two-tower lead, Baron/base access, or impaired trailing protection:

- central projection at least three above line;
- lower branch no more than two below;
- two trailing validated return-kill channels;
- no clean-close veto.

A range that barely straddles the line is at most `LEAN`.

## Kill Under restraint

Formal Under prohibited when:

- at least three unresolved inventory categories remain;
- both teams retain two validated channels;
- no clean-close route removes a cycle;
- line is within two kills of the central projection.

Substantial line-specific headroom may still qualify if the upper conservative budget and safety buffer are satisfied.

## Duration quarantine

All Duration Over and Duration Under markets are official-ineligible through wager 20.

Maximum status is `LEAN` or `WATCH ONLY` and every verdict must append:

`ANALYSIS ONLY — DURATION INELIGIBLE THROUGH WAGER 20`

Duration analysis remains mandatory.

## One-sequence close score

At 20:00+, score the leader:

1. two item-qualified channels;
2. objective leverage/next-neutral control;
3. tower access/exposed lane/Baron siege;
4. impaired defender clear/carry/frontline/protection;
5. engage/flank/backline access/reset denial.

Treatment:

- 0-2: no automatic compression;
- 3: shorten central duration one to two minutes;
- 4: no Duration Over lean unless earliest finish is at least three minutes beyond line;
- 5: Duration Over `NO BET`; test one-sequence or one-reset finish.

Prior Baron recovery adds time only if safe waves, vision, items, and structure defense were restored.

## Moneyline preference

Prefer moneyline over an excessive negative handicap when the side has independent kill/tower/objective confirmation, two functioning channels, superior protection/access, and the opponent has an impaired core channel.
