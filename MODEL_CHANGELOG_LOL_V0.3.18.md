# LoL Model Changelog v0.3.18

**Effective:** 2026-08-02 11:02 UTC+7

## Trigger

The probation-period lean/watch review reached 44 graded snapshots: 21-23 and -1.1455u at a hypothetical 0.25u each. Multiple snapshots were revisions of the same map thesis and are not independent bets.

The review showed:

- weak moneyline leans from draft plus soft early evidence;
- repeated handicap failures after underdog damage or map function collapsed;
- poor draft-only and early-state kill Overs;
- strong late kill Overs when actual fight velocity and item strength were present;
- strong wide kill cushions only when return damage remained functional.

## Changes

- Added a minimum live-moneyline lean gate: one hard confirmation, or two independent soft confirmations without a major team-quality disadvantage.
- Against a substantial team gap, a hard confirmation is required even for a moneyline lean.
- Added a functional-return-damage gate and conservative next-fight stress test for positive-underdog kill handicaps.
- Added a same-map thesis withdrawal and re-entry lockout.
- Re-entry now requires two new structured-fight conversions, stabilization, restored item-qualified function, and repair of the original failure condition.
- Kill-Over leans now require observed two-sided conversion or demonstrated high kill velocity; draft engage density alone is prohibited.
- Added explicit return-kill production analysis to kill Unders.
- Added thesis-level tracking so revised lines are not treated as independent model evidence.
- Duration Overs are suspended from official eligibility for probation wagers 7–10.

## Unchanged

- 1u = 1,000,000 VND.
- Standard stake and active per-map cap = 0.25u = 250,000 VND.
- Minimum odds = 1.60.
- Every changed line requires independent reassessment.
- v0.3.17 official underdog-moneyline confirmation rules remain active.
- Positive-underdog kill handicaps still require two qualifying structured-fight conversions for official promotion.
- Unplaced, placed-from-lean, and strict model-approved results remain separated.
