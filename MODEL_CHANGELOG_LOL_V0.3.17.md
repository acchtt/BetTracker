# LoL Model Changelog v0.3.17

**Effective:** 2026-08-02 02:33 UTC+7

## Trigger

KC vs NAVI Game 1: NAVI ML was promoted at 10:46 from a 2-0 kill lead, approximately +0.6k gold, and 3-0 Void Grubs with towers and dragons still 0-0. NAVI later reached a strong winning position but failed to convert and lost.

## Changes

- Split moneyline evidence into soft and hard confirmations.
- Void Grubs alone are now soft confirmation.
- Kills plus the immediate resulting gold remain one correlated cluster.
- Underdog official moneylines require at least one hard conversion confirmation.
- Against a substantial team-quality gap, require two hard confirmations or one hard plus two additional independent confirmations.
- Added mandatory lead-conversion reliability and next-two-sequence tests.
- Added explicit favorite-comeback and throw-branch analysis.
- Attractive price cannot replace durable structural conversion.

## Unchanged

- 1u = 1,000,000 VND.
- Standard stake 0.25u = 250,000 VND.
- Minimum odds 1.60.
- Probation map exposure cap 0.25u.
- Existing draft, item, duration, kill-total, handicap, expiry, and status-language rules.
