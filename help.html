HOW IT WORKS

Recommended workflow

  1. 1
    Add a manual bet from the Bets page or open the Betslip Parser.
  2. 2
    Confirm event, market, odds, stake, and status before saving.
  3. 3
    Settle pending bets when results are confirmed.
  4. 4
    Review Analytics and Bankroll separately from daily entry.
  5. 5
    Export a JSON backup regularly to protect your local data.

PRIVACY

Local-first storage

EdgeLog stores bets in your browser using localStorage. GitHub Pages does not receive your betting history.

SUPPORTED INPUT

Betslip parser

The parser recognizes common Vietnamese and English labels for event, league, odds, stake, payout, status, result, and date.
