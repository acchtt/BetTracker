# League of Legends Model Rules v0.3.17

**Status:** Active immediately  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-02 02:33 UTC+7  
**Purpose:** Strengthen live underdog-moneyline promotion after KC vs NAVI Game 1.

This rule supersedes earlier moneyline-confirmation language where conflicting. Draft, item, kill-market, duration, expiry, stake, objective-reading, and probation rules remain active where not superseded.

## 1. Triggering review

At 10:46 in KC vs NAVI Game 1, NAVI led 2-0 in kills, approximately 0.6k gold, and 3-0 in Void Grubs, while towers and dragons remained 0-0. NAVI ML was promoted to an official recommendation and accepted at 2.153 for 0.25u. NAVI later built a substantially stronger winning position but failed to convert, and KC won.

The entry error was not that the early state had no value. The error was treating a modest kill/economy cluster plus low-commitment Grub control as sufficiently durable confirmation for an underdog moneyline against a stronger favorite.

## 2. Confirmation hierarchy

Moneyline evidence is now divided into **soft** and **hard** confirmation.

### Soft confirmation

Soft evidence can support a lean but normally cannot complete an underdog official-bet gate by itself:

- a single kill or first-blood sequence;
- the small gold lead created by that same sequence;
- Void Grubs without meaningful structure conversion;
- isolated CS or lane-priority advantages;
- draft edge without current-map execution;
- one unconverted objective setup;
- one opponent mistake without a repeatable control pattern.

Kills and the resulting immediate gold lead remain one correlated confirmation cluster.

### Hard confirmation

Hard evidence demonstrates durable conversion or repeatable control:

- a tower lead or meaningful structure conversion;
- a dragon lead with maintained setup control;
- Herald converted into material plates, tower damage, or a tower;
- a second structured-fight win after a reset or objective-cycle separation;
- a meaningful, usable item breakpoint on primary damage or frontline combined with map control;
- repeated wave, vision, and terrain control that denies the opponent a normal contest;
- a gold lead large and well distributed enough to remain meaningful after one lost skirmish.

Void Grubs alone are not hard confirmation.

## 3. Underdog live-moneyline gate

An underdog live moneyline may be `OFFICIAL BET` only when:

1. at least two independent current-map confirmations are present;
2. at least one confirmation is hard;
3. the evidence is not merely kills plus their resulting gold plus Grubs;
4. the underdog has a credible next-two-sequence conversion path;
5. the favorite's comeback routes are explicitly priced;
6. current composition functionality and item strength are checked when required by clock;
7. team/player lead-conversion reliability is not a material unresolved negative.

When the pre-match market, series context, or observed play indicates a substantial team-quality gap, require either:

- two hard confirmations; or
- one hard confirmation plus two additional independent confirmations, including a post-reset fight or objective conversion.

Otherwise, cap the recommendation at `LEAN — 0u watch`.

## 4. Lead-conversion reliability check

Before promoting an underdog moneyline, state:

- how the underdog converts the current lead into the next tower, dragon, Herald, Baron setup, or item breakpoint;
- whether the lead is concentrated on champions able to carry the next fight;
- whether the underdog has already shown disciplined objective setup, wave preparation, and reset timing;
- whether the favorite has superior late-game damage delivery, side-lane pressure, engage reliability, or player execution capable of reversing a small lead;
- the most realistic throw branch.

Generic statements such as “better draft” or “three damage sources” are insufficient without this conversion review.

## 5. Next-two-sequence test

For an actionable underdog moneyline, project the next two likely sequences.

The recommendation is `NO BET` when the most likely path is:

- underdog takes a low-value objective but concedes tower or dragon;
- underdog cannot convert lane priority into structures;
- favorite reaches a major item breakpoint before the next mandatory contest;
- one lost fight erases the entire lead;
- underdog must repeatedly out-execute a stronger favorite without structural insulation.

## 6. Pricing consequences

- A soft-confirmation underdog state may justify a fair-price adjustment or lean.
- Official promotion requires the hard-confirmation gate above.
- Attractive odds do not compensate for missing durable conversion.
- If the underdog later gains hard confirmation, reassess the current price independently.
- No recommendation survives a material state or price change without the existing expiry checks.

## 7. User-facing fast output

For underdog moneylines, the immediate verdict should identify:

- current clock and state;
- soft confirmations;
- hard confirmations;
- lead-conversion path;
- favorite comeback route;
- quoted odds, target floor, hard floor, stake, and placement status when actionable.

If no hard confirmation exists, return the verdict immediately rather than over-analysing secondary markets.
