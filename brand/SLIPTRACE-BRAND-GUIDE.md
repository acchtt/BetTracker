# SlipTrace Brand Guide

**Product name:** SlipTrace  
**Tagline:** Track every slip.

## Core mark

The primary symbol is a compact white geometric **S** inside an open circular trace. The ring runs from violet through electric blue to cyan and retains a narrow opening at three o'clock.

Use:

- `assets/sliptrace-mark.svg` for the sidebar and standalone symbol
- `assets/sliptrace-app-icon.svg` for favicons, PWA installation, and social avatars
- `assets/sliptrace-horizontal-dark.svg` for dark-background marketing lockups

## Palette

- Violet: `#7C3AED`
- Electric blue: `#2563FF`
- Cyan: `#00D4FF`
- Navy: `#0B1220`
- Deep navy: `#07101F`
- Slate: `#94A3B8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Primary gradient:

```css
linear-gradient(135deg, #7C3AED 0%, #2563FF 48%, #00D4FF 100%)
```

## Typography

Use Inter or a clean system sans-serif for the application. The wordmark is uppercase with restrained letter spacing:

- `SLIP` in white or navy
- `TRACE` in blue-to-cyan gradient

## Clear space

Keep empty space around the symbol equal to at least one quarter of the ring diameter.

## Minimum sizes

- Standalone mark: 24 px
- App icon: 32 px
- Horizontal lockup: 180 px wide

## Avoid

- Closing or moving the ring gap
- Enlarging the S until it touches the ring
- Thickening the ring
- Adding bevels, glows, embossing, or internal shadows
- Stretching or rotating the mark
- Separating the gradient into unrelated colors

## Brand voice

SlipTrace should sound clear, disciplined, calm, and data-focused. Avoid exaggerated claims or casino-style language.
