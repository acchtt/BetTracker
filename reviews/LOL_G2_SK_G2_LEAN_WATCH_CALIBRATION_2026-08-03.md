## Calibration lessons

1. Keep positive-handicap evaluation tied to the remaining future net-kill requirement, structural state, and return-kill capacity—not the printed line alone.
2. Treat prolonged structural parity as positive for duration Over and potentially positive for Kill Over when both teams retain damage and engage.
3. Do not use strong waveclear as automatic Kill Under evidence; it can extend the game into additional violent objective cycles.
4. Early Under watches at 32.5 and 34.5 were false directions because unresolved map inventory remained too large.
5. Over 31.5 at 22:09 was a legitimate official recommendation even though the market locked immediately after the verdict.
6. Market locking is an execution outcome, not a model failure. Record verdict validity, execution availability, and placement separately.
7. The placed Over 49.5 was correctly promoted only after a fresh synchronized reassessment at the improved execution price.
