## Model changes

### 1. Representative-thesis accounting

Repeated reprices count as one continuous thesis until a material state transition. Price changes alone are not new observations.

### 2. Winner versus margin separation

Moneyline evidence cannot automatically support a large negative handicap. Margin requires independent future net-kill evidence.

### 3. Validated return-kill channels

Late Kill Over promotion requires damage plus executable delivery, protection, frontline, engage, or target access. Nominal farmed carries are insufficient.

### 4. Clean-close suppression

Added:

`NO BET — CLEAN-CLOSE/RETURN-KILL SUPPRESSION VETO`

This blocks Overs when the leader has structural access and multiple conversion channels while the trailing side lacks validated return-kill functionality.

### 5. Leader-controlled Over margins

In leader-controlled states, central projection must clear the line by at least three, lower branch may be no more than two below, and the trailing side must retain two validated return-kill channels.

### 6. Kill Under restraint

Under is blocked when large unresolved map inventory, two-sided functionality, and a line near the central estimate coexist.

### 7. Duration quarantine

All duration markets are analysis-only through probation wager 20. The reviewed Duration WATCH/LEAN record was 5-15.

### 8. One-sequence close score

After 20:00, explicitly score item-qualified damage, objective leverage, tower access, defender impairment, and engage/backline access before projecting Duration Over.
