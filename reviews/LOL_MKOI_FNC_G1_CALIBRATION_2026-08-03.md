## Process lessons

1. Repeated kill parity protects wider positive handicaps more reliably than tighter ones when the opponent has a growing multi-carry economy.
2. A prior Baron recovery should not automatically add a full objective cycle to duration when the recovering side still lacks stable wave control and one core damage channel is impaired.
3. For Duration Over after 22:00, explicitly score the favorite's one-sequence close path: objective control, two item-qualified carries, tower access and defender waveclear quality.
4. Keep kill-total and duration projections separate. This map produced enough late kills to clear 37.5 while still ending before 33 minutes.
5. Wager 9 was a valid placed win, but its narrow margin should reduce confidence in similarly thin Over thresholds.
